SCOPE / WHAT THIS BUILD ACTUALLY PROVES

Credibility is a product feature.

A working sandbox with real control boundaries—not a claim of production completeness.

01 / AGENTIC JUDGMENT

One live reasoning path.

The cloud Supervisor uses Gemini and constrained ADK tools. Local recovery uses deterministic fixtures. Vendor, Invoice and Treasury currently execute fixture-driven workflows; their native runtime identities do not mean each case invokes three models.

02 / BUSINESS EFFECTS

Synthetic, reversible work.

Vendor, invoice and payment records really change in the sandbox. No money moves. The irreversible bank-export escalation is a synthetic fixture, not a real bank integration. Recovery enumerates the current case, not every historical case across the tenant.

03 / AUTHORIZATION

Enforcement, not enterprise IAM.

TENURE enforces capability authority at the mutation boundary. Native Google Agent Gateway is not deployed. Tenant-keyed records are not a substitute for authenticated multi-tenant authorization. Cloud access remains IAM-protected.

04 / DURABILITY

Know where the boundary ends.

Cloud authority and sandbox records persist in Firestore. This local fleet uses shared memory. Business mutations and audit receipts are separate commits. Crashed owners require reconciliation; automatic crash recovery is not claimed.

05 / EVALUATION

500 cases, not 500 customers.

Twenty parameterized synthetic families test policy and control behavior. They do not establish Gemini reliability, real-world attack prevalence, human time savings, or production financial risk. The human baseline is modeled.

06 / STILL TO PROVE

The unfinished work is visible.

Interactive durable wake and native identity controls, automatic incident-memory learning, measured model tokens and costs, deployed UI verification and a cold-reader walkthrough remain. No prize outcome is guaranteed.
